# AI Risk Review

Use this module before launch, autonomy expansion, model/tool changes, or external release. Anchor governance in NIST AI RMF-style Map, Measure, Manage, and Govern functions.

## Map

- Intended use, users, affected stakeholders
- Context, domain, autonomy, and decision impact
- Data, model, prompt, retrieval, tool, and skill surfaces
- Known harms and misuse cases

## Measure

- Quality, robustness, fairness/segment, privacy, security, safety, explainability, and cost metrics
- Red-team cases and adversarial tests
- Tool side-effect and approval compliance

## Manage

- Mitigations, human oversight, escalation, rollback, access controls
- Incident response and monitoring
- Release/canary policy

## Govern

- Owners, approvals, audit logs, policies, documentation, review cadence
- Required artifacts: PRD, dataset card, model card, eval report, release gate, monitoring spec

## Output

Produce risk tier, blockers, mitigations, approval checklist, and evidence gaps.
