# Product Discovery and PRD

Use this module before architecture/model decisions. Applied science begins with the workflow and decision being improved.

## Product discovery workflow

1. Identify primary users, affected customers, workflow moments, and current human/system baseline.
2. Define jobs-to-be-improved and decisions-to-support.
3. State autonomy level: assist, draft, recommend, execute-with-approval, bounded autonomous, or autonomous.
4. Define non-goals and disallowed actions.
5. Define success metrics, guardrails, and unacceptable failures.
6. Map required data/context/tools and their permission boundaries.
7. Create a baseline ladder before selecting AI complexity.

## PRD sections

- Problem and user pain
- Current workflow and baseline
- Target users and stakeholders
- Jobs-to-be-done
- Scope and non-goals
- Autonomy and approval boundaries
- Required data/context/tool surfaces
- Success metrics and guardrails
- UX/output requirements
- Evaluation plan
- Launch and monitoring plan
- Risks, mitigations, and open questions

## Applied science checks

- Does the metric match the real user/workflow outcome?
- Could a process/template/search/classifier baseline solve it?
- Is the data available at inference time with permission?
- What failure would block launch regardless of average quality?
